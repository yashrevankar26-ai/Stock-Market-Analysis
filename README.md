# Stock-Market-Analysis
A comprehensive Stock Market Analytics Dashboard project that leverages SQL, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau to analyze stock prices, trading volume, and market trends. The project delivers interactive dashboards and data-driven insights to support informed investment decisions, performance tracking, and risk management.

#Project Overview
This project presents a unified view of stock market data by converting large and complex datasets into visually clear and interactive dashboards. It enables users to quickly understand market trends, stock performance, and sector behavior without deep technical analysis. The dashboards help investors and analysts identify opportunities, compare performance, and track key market indicators in an easy and efficient way.

#Objectives
*Provide a simplified and consolidated view of stock market performance
*Enable quick identification of trends, patterns, and anomalies
*Support faster and more informed investment decision-making
*Reduce analysis time through intuitive visual dashboards

#Tools & Technologies
*Excel: Data preparation and structured analysis
*SQL: Data extraction and transformation
*Power BI: Interactive dashboards and KPI visualization
*Tableau: Visual storytelling and comparative analysis

#Key Insights
*Clear identification of top and underperforming stocks
*Easy comparison of sector-wise market performance
*Quick visualization of price movements, volume trends, and market behavior
*Simplified view of factors influencing stock performance

#Outcomes
*User-friendly dashboards enabling fast and effective stock market analysis
*Improved ability to interpret market data at a glance
*Streamlined analysis process for investors and analysts
*Better support for data-driven investment decisions
